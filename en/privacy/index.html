Summary

  • QbFX has no account or login and the developer does not receive or store your presets, setlists or settings: everything stays on your device.
  • The AI assistant sends the text you type (and some context) to a third-party service, DeepSeek, to generate the tone.
  • The free edition shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects data to serve and measure ads. The Pro edition has no ads and does not include the ads SDK.

Who we are

QbFX is developed by Bruno Gama Catão, an individual developer. To talk about this policy, use GitHub.

Data that stays on your device

Presets, setlists, app preferences and the AI-generation counter used by the ad rules are stored only in the device's local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it. When you export a preset or the library, the file goes wherever you choose (a file, the clipboard or the system share sheet).

AI assistant (DeepSeek)

When you use the assistant, the app sends over HTTPS to api.deepseek.com (a service run by DeepSeek, with servers outside Brazil, including in China):

  • the text you typed and the recent messages of the conversation (up to 12 messages of up to 600 characters each);
  • a summary of the preset being edited (up to 1,000 characters) and the device language, so it can answer in your language.

The app does not send your name, e-mail, device identifier or location. The provider still receives the IP address of your connection, as with any internet access. Do not type personal or sensitive data into the assistant. DeepSeek handles this data under its own privacy policy, published on its official website (deepseek.com). If you do not use the assistant, none of this is sent.

Ads (free edition)

The free edition uses Google AdMob to show a banner and rewarded videos. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language) and ad interaction data to serve, cap the frequency of, measure and prevent fraud in ads. This can include an approximate location estimated from the IP.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can review your choice in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • See how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The QbFX Pro edition contains no ads and no AdMob SDK and does not use the advertising ID.

Android permissions

InternetThe AI assistant and ads (free edition).

FilesExporting and importing presets and backups through the system file picker, only for the files you pick.

Who the data is shared with

Only with the two services above: DeepSeek (if you use the AI assistant) and Google AdMob (free edition). The developer does not sell data and does not use it to build profiles.

Security

Communication with DeepSeek and Google uses HTTPS. Local data is kept in the app's private area on Android.

Children

QbFX is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Because the developer does not keep your data, you control what is on the device (removing the app or its data deletes everything). For data handled by DeepSeek or Google (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability under the LGPD, the GDPR and similar laws), use those services' own channels. If you need help, reach us on GitHub.

Changes to this policy

When the app or its data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. Do not put personal data in the description: it is public.
