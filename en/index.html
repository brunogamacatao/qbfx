What QbFX does

Live mode

Tweak amp, delay, cab/IR and reverb in real time and switch between presets A, B and C with big buttons made for the stage.

Preset editor

Build presets and write them to the pedal, import cabinet IRs (.wav) and back up the 8 IR slots.

Setlists

Organize songs, each with its own A/B/C presets, and get the current song ready on the pedal.

AI assistant

Describe the tone you want (for example, “Slash's solo tone”) and get a preset to open in the editor, try and save.

Connection

USB-MIDI straight to the pedal or, when the phone is busy, a Bluetooth/network bridge to control the pedal from another device.

Backup and sharing

Export and import presets and the whole library through a file, the clipboard or the system share sheet.
